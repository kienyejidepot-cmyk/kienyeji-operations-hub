People
- Who is responsible at each shop for stock counts, cash reconciliation, credit follow-up, and closing the day?
- Can each staff member explain how a mistake, a dead bird, or an unrecorded sale affects profit?
- Which decisions can staff make themselves, and which need a manager’s approval?
Product
- Which chicken types, sizes, cuts, or customer orders produce the best margin per bird?
- What is your true cost per chicken by the time it sells, including purchase price, transport, holding, water, electricity, and labour?
- At what point does keeping a chicken past three days cost more than the extra selling price can recover?
- Which products or customers look popular but contribute little profit after their full costs?
Process
- Can you trace a chicken from supplier and purchase batch through shop stock to sale, death, return, or adjustment?
- How often does recorded stock differ from the count in the shop or freezer, and what usually explains the difference?
- How are deaths recorded, valued, and investigated by batch, shop, and cause?
- Can a sale or expense be posted quickly by staff, with its supporting photo and the person who entered it?
- How do you know a customer’s M-Pesa payment was received and applied to the correct sale?
Numbers and cash
- How many chickens did you buy, sell, lose, and still have in stock last week at each shop?
- What are your gross profit and net profit per chicken, by shop and product?
- What is the minimum selling price that covers the chicken’s full cost and a fair share of operating costs?
- How much of your revenue is still unpaid, how old is that debt, and which customers are overdue?
- Which expenses are rising fastest, and which shop has the highest costs per chicken sold?
- What is your break-even sales volume for each shop?
- How much cash is available today across cash tills, bank accounts, and M-Pesa, after outstanding obligations?
- Are purchases, expenses, stock losses, owner withdrawals, investor capital, and loans recorded separately?
Growth and accountability
- Which shop is earning a return on the cash and stock invested in it?
- If you added money to the business, what specific improvement would it fund, and how would you measure the result?
- Which issue should be fixed first: people, process, product mix, pricing, or cash collection?
- What would you stop, change, or test over the next 30 days, and who owns that action?
